Return & Exchange Policy

At Krishna Fashion, customer satisfaction is important to us

Effective Date: January 2025

We Accept Exchange Only If

  • Wrong product received — item does not match your order
  • Damaged product received — visible damage upon delivery
  • Manufacturing defect — fault in stitching, fabric, or finishing

Mandatory Conditions

  • Exchange request within 3 days of delivery — requests after this window will not be accepted
  • Unboxing video required — a clear video of the parcel being opened is mandatory for all exchange claims
  • Product must be unused and unwashed, with all original tags and packaging intact

We Do Not Accept Exchange / Return For

  • Size issues — please refer to the size guide before ordering
  • Slight color variation — minor differences due to screen display settings
  • Sale items — products purchased during discount sales are final
  • Used or washed items — products that have been worn or laundered

Important: Always record an unboxing video before opening your parcel. Without a valid unboxing video, we will not be able to process any exchange request, even for damaged or wrong items.

How to Initiate an Exchange

1

Record Video

2
